Is ERA5 the best free temperature data in Quebec?

Not for daily highs. At 21 weather stations in Quebec, 2017–2024, ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 12 of 21 stations; typical errors: ECMWF IFS 1.20 °C, ERA5 1.33 °C. ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 16 of 21 stations. For rain, NASA POWER had the smallest error in weekly rain at 16 of 21 stations.

Quebec up close

What stands out

  • ERA5's error in the daily high ranged from 0.70 °C at Val-D'Or to 1.91 °C at Cap-Tourmente, where the ERA5 grid point sits 251 m above the station.
  • ERA5's typical error in the daily high was 1.33 °C here and 1.12 °C across the whole Canada edition; NASA POWER's weekly rain error was 5.4 mm here and 4.6 mm across the edition.
  • In the growing season, April to September, weekly rain was off by 6.8 mm in NASA POWER, 9.1 mm in ERA5 and 8.8 mm in ECMWF IFS.
  • Of the days NASA POWER showed rain (1 mm or more), 27% were dry at the station; for ERA5 it was 25%.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Reference: the daily records of Environment and Climate Change Canada, with each day counted the way the service counts it (highs, lows and rain from 06:00 to 06:00 UTC). Every source is rebuilt into the same days.
  • Each source is read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation. All sources are compared on the same days, 2017–2024; ECMWF IFS is Open-Meteo's archive of the 9 km forecast model, which starts in 2017.
  • Many of these stations send their readings to the forecast centers, and ERA5 and the ECMWF forecasts use them. NASA POWER rain is corrected with gauge data. In a field between stations, errors may be larger.
  • Full method, maps and the farm-task tables for the whole edition: Canada edition.
