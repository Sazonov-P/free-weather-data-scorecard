Is ERA5-Land more accurate than ERA5?

Not at weather stations. ERA5-Land's grid is finer (0.1° against ERA5's 0.25°) and its grid points sat closer to the stations, yet at 804 stations in the US, Europe, Canada and Australia, 2017–2024, its error in the daily high was larger than ERA5's in 28 of 29 regions and its error in the daily low in all 29. ERA5-Land had the lower error at 132 of 804 stations for the daily high and 123 of 804 for the daily low.

Why a finer grid did not help

Closer grid points, larger errors

At the median station, the nearest ERA5-Land grid point was 3.9 km away and 16 m off the station's height; for ERA5 it was 9.9 km and 30 m. A closer grid point did not make ERA5-Land's daily values closer to what the stations measured.

We do not claim a cause. ERA5-Land re-runs the land part of ERA5 at a finer grid, driven by ERA5's weather; we checked only its daily highs and lows at stations, not its soil moisture, snow or rain.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Stations: national weather services of the US, seven European countries, Canada and Australia, each day counted the way the service counts it. Regions with at least 10 stations.
  • Both sources read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation, on the same days, 2017–2024.
  • Each edition explains its stations and day boundaries: US, Europe, Canada, Australia.
