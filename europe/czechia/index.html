Is ERA5 the best free temperature data in Czechia?

It shares the lead on daily highs. At 20 weather stations in Czechia, 2017–2024, ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 8 of 20 stations; typical errors: ERA5 1.33 °C, ECMWF IFS 1.37 °C. ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 14 of 20 stations. For rain, NASA POWER had the smallest error in weekly rain at 13 of 20 stations.

Czechia up close

What stands out

  • ERA5's error in the daily high ranged from 0.70 °C at Zbýšov, Dobrovítov to 2.96 °C at Teplice, where the ERA5 grid point sits 248 m above the station.
  • ERA5's typical error in the daily high was 1.33 °C here and 1.17 °C across the whole European edition; NASA POWER's weekly rain error was 5.4 mm here and 5.5 mm across the edition.
  • In the growing season, April to September, weekly rain was off by 7.1 mm in NASA POWER, 8.1 mm in ERA5 and 7.4 mm in ECMWF IFS.
  • Of the days NASA POWER showed rain (1 mm or more), 33% were dry at the station; for ERA5 it was 33%.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Reference: the daily records of the Czech Hydrometeorological Institute (ČHMÚ), with each day counted the way the service counts it (highs and lows from 20:00 UTC the day before to 20:00 UTC, rain from 06:00 to 06:00 UTC). Every source is rebuilt into the same days.
  • Each source is read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation. All sources are compared on the same days, 2017–2024; ECMWF IFS is Open-Meteo's archive of the 9 km forecast model, which starts in 2017.
  • Many of these stations send their readings to the forecast centers, and ERA5 and the ECMWF forecasts use them. NASA POWER rain is corrected with gauge data. In a field between stations, errors may be larger.
  • Full method, maps and the farm-task tables for the whole edition: European edition.
