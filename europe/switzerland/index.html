Accuracy check · 12 weather stations in Switzerland · 2017–2024

Is ERA5 the best free temperature data in Switzerland?

It shares the lead on daily highs. At 12 weather stations in Switzerland, 2017–2024, ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 6 of 12 stations; typical errors: ERA5 1.97 °C, ECMWF IFS 2.49 °C. ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily low, each with the smallest error at 5 of 12 stations. For rain, NASA POWER had the smallest error in weekly rain at 9 of 12 stations.

What stands out

  • ERA5's error in the daily high ranged from 0.71 °C at Bern / Zollikofen to 6.93 °C at Cevio, where the ERA5 grid point sits 1030 m above the station.
  • ERA5's typical error in the daily high was 1.97 °C here and 1.17 °C across the whole European edition; NASA POWER's weekly rain error was 9.0 mm here and 5.5 mm across the edition.
  • In the growing season, April to September, weekly rain was off by 10.5 mm in NASA POWER, 13.2 mm in ERA5 and 12.7 mm in ECMWF IFS.
  • Of the days NASA POWER showed rain (1 mm or more), 33% were dry at the station; for ERA5 it was 32%.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Reference: the daily records of MeteoSwiss, with each day counted the way the service counts it (highs, lows and rain over 00:00–24:00 UTC). Every source is rebuilt into the same days.
  • Each source is read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation. All sources are compared on the same days, 2017–2024; ECMWF IFS is Open-Meteo's archive of the 9 km forecast model, which starts in 2017.
  • Many of these stations send their readings to the forecast centers, and ERA5 and the ECMWF forecasts use them. NASA POWER rain is corrected with gauge data. In a field between stations, errors may be larger.
  • Full method, maps and the farm-task tables for the whole edition: European edition.
