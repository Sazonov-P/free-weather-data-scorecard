Is ERA5 the best free temperature data in Tasmania?

A close race on daily highs. At 14 weather stations in Tasmania, 2017–2024, ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 8 of 14 stations; typical errors: ERA5 1.78 °C, ECMWF IFS 1.79 °C. ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily low, each with the smallest error at 6 of 14 stations. For rain, NASA POWER had the smallest error in weekly rain at 8 of 14 stations.

Tasmania up close

What stands out

  • ERA5's error in the daily high ranged from 0.99 °C at Flinders Island Airport to 4.14 °C at Ouse Fire Station, where the ERA5 grid point sits 431 m above the station.
  • ERA5's typical error in the daily high was 1.78 °C here and 1.31 °C across the whole Australian edition; NASA POWER's weekly rain error was 6.5 mm here and 5.2 mm across the edition.
  • In the growing season, October to March, weekly rain was off by 6.4 mm in NASA POWER, 6.0 mm in ERA5 and 7.1 mm in ECMWF IFS.
  • Of the days NASA POWER showed rain (1 mm or more), 33% were dry at the station; for ERA5 it was 31%.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Reference: the daily records of the Bureau of Meteorology, with each day counted the way the service counts it (rain and daily lows over the 24 hours to 9 a.m. local time, daily highs over the 24 hours from 9 a.m.). Every source is rebuilt into the same days.
  • Each source is read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation. All sources are compared on the same days, 2017–2024; ECMWF IFS is Open-Meteo's archive of the 9 km forecast model, which starts in 2017.
  • Many of these stations send their readings to the forecast centers, and ERA5 and the ECMWF forecasts use them. NASA POWER rain is corrected with gauge data. In a field between stations, errors may be larger.
  • Full method, maps and the farm-task tables for the whole edition: Australian edition.
