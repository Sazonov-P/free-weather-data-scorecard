How accurate is ERA5 weather data?

The most accurate free source for daily temperature in most places. At 807 weather stations in the US, Europe, Canada and Australia, 2017–2024, ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily high at 471 of 804 stations and in the daily low at 533 of 804; its typical daily-high error ranged from 1.06 °C (US) to 1.31 °C (Australia). For weekly rain it won 156 of 807 stations, behind NASA POWER, but led in Denmark, Alberta and South Australia. Its largest errors were at valley stations far below the grid point.

Exceptions

Where ERA5 is not the clear winner on temperature

  • California: ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily high at 10 of 17 stations; ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily low at 10 of 17 stations.
  • Washington State: ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily high at 8 of 14 stations; ERA5 and NASA POWER tied on the daily low, each with the smallest error at 5 of 14 stations.
  • Switzerland: ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 6 of 12 stations; ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily low, each with the smallest error at 5 of 12 stations.
  • Czechia: ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 8 of 20 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 14 of 20 stations.
  • Denmark: ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in both the daily high and the daily low at 9 of 17 stations.
  • Quebec: ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 12 of 21 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 16 of 21 stations.
  • Northern Territory: ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 10 of 24 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 13 of 24 stations.
  • Queensland: NASA POWER had the smallest error in the daily high at 22 of 53 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 36 of 53 stations.
  • South Australia: ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 12 of 32 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 24 of 32 stations.
  • Tasmania: ECMWF IFS had the smallest error in the daily high at 8 of 14 stations; ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily low, each with the smallest error at 6 of 14 stations.
  • Victoria: ERA5 and ECMWF IFS tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 10 of 26 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 16 of 26 stations.
  • Western Australia: ERA5 and NASA POWER tied on the daily high, each with the smallest error at 17 of 49 stations; ERA5 had the smallest error in the daily low at 30 of 49 stations.

Method and limits

How we checked

  • Stations: the national weather services of the US, seven European countries, Canada and Australia, each day counted the way the service counts it. Regions with at least 10 stations get their own page.
  • Each source is read at the nearest grid point over land, without interpolation, on the same days for all sources, 2017–2024. Typical error is the mean absolute error at the median station: of daily values for temperature, of weekly totals for rain.
  • Many of these stations send their readings to the forecast centers, and ERA5 and the ECMWF forecasts use them; NASA POWER rain is corrected with gauge data. In a field between stations, errors may be larger.
  • Each edition explains its stations and day boundaries: US, Europe, Canada, Australia.
